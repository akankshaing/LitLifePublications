Keeping the Reading Habit Alive

The Lit Life Reading Movement

Publishing books is only one part of our purpose. Through the Movement, we bring books closer to people, and people closer to ideas.

Reading Circles

Community-based and digital reading groups where people read, reflect, and learn together.

Read Every Day Campaign

A simple movement encouraging individuals and families to dedicate a few uninterrupted minutes to reading daily.

Books Beyond Classrooms

Introducing students to literature, ideas, biographies, and life skills beyond prescribed textbooks.

Author–Reader Conversations

Interactive sessions where readers engage directly with the authors behind the books.

Young Readers Programme

Storytelling sessions, book clubs, and writing workshops for children and teenagers.

Workplace Reading Culture

Book clubs, leadership reading programmes, and libraries introduced into workplaces.

Community Libraries

Supporting the establishment and revival of small libraries in schools and underserved areas.

Gift a Book Initiative

Encouraging individuals and organisations to gift books on birthdays, festivals, and milestones.

One Book, Many Lives

A book-sharing initiative through which one meaningful book travels across many readers.

Our Commitment to Young Minds

The future of reading depends upon the habits we build today. We work with schools, teachers, parents, libraries, and youth organisations to make reading enjoyable rather than compulsory.

Our objective is not merely to create better students — it is to create curious minds, confident communicators, empathetic human beings, and independent thinkers.

Open a book. Open your mind. Open a new world.
