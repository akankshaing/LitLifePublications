Our Commitment to Authors
  • We promise to treat every manuscript with dignity
  • We promise to communicate honestly
  • We promise not to compromise quality for speed
  • We promise to protect originality and intellectual effort
  • We promise to create partnerships rather than transactions
  • We promise to help authors present their finest work to the world

We may not publish every manuscript we receive, but every manuscript is viewed as the result of someone's imagination, experience, effort, and courage.

Submit Your Manuscript

Send us your manuscript or proposal along with the details below, and our editorial team will begin its evaluation.

  • A brief author profile
  • Book title and synopsis
  • Proposed chapter outline
  • Target readership

  • The purpose and central message of the book
