Our Publishing Process

Eight steps, from manuscript to launch.

01

Manuscript Submission

The author submits the manuscript, synopsis, profile, and relevant supporting information.

02

Editorial Evaluation

Our editorial team assesses quality, relevance, structure, originality, and publishing potential.

03

Publishing Discussion

We discuss editorial requirements, publishing model, timelines, and the proposed roadmap.

04

Editing & Development

The manuscript undergoes the agreed editorial process to strengthen language, structure, and flow.

05

Design & Formatting

Cover, interior layout, typography, and overall visual identity are developed.

06

Author Review

The author reviews the edited and designed version before final approval.

07

Production & Publication

The book is prepared for print, digital, or other agreed formats.

08

Launch & Promotion

A customised launch, distribution, and promotional plan tailored to the book's audience.
